Hailey's Learning Land

Learning through play.

Hailey's Learning Land is a free iPhone, iPad and Android app with fourteen learning games for young children. A friendly voice reads every instruction aloud, so even children who can't read yet can play on their own.

  • Magic Letters. Trace letters with a finger and watch them come alive.
  • Word Detective. Find sight words like "the", "see" and "my".
  • Count & Collect. Feed hungry animals, find numbers and try simple addition.
  • Art Studio. Draw with crayons, markers and glitter, add stamps, and choose from 100 coloring pages in six themes.
  • Flip & Find. A memory game with animals, treats and magical things.
  • Sound Safari. Match letters to the sounds that begin words.
  • Puzzle World. Shape sorters and jigsaw puzzles.
  • Make a Friend. Dress up a friend and decorate a magical place.
  • Build a Story. Pick a hero, a place and a magical object, draw a picture, and hear the story read aloud.
  • Little Chef. Count ingredients, sort fruit by color, and follow first, next and last steps.
  • Shape Quest. Hunt for shapes and colors, then build a house, a robot or a rocket.
  • Music Maker. Play a xylophone and drums, copy little tunes, and sing the alphabet song.
  • My Little Pet. Care for a puppy, kitten, unicorn or bunny by counting, reading and drawing.

Every game is free and unlocked from the start. There are no ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts and no tracking, and everything works offline. Games get a little harder as your child gets the hang of them, and a little easier if they need help.
